The deal

Every event publishes its prize pool before it opens. Cash, credits, or tools — whatever the sponsors put up. You always know what you're competing for.

Finish anything and you get a certificate with your name on it. Place in the top three and your project gets its own page on this site, linked from the homepage.

Who's running it. A high schooler in Washington. Themes come from what students ask for, not from a marketing team's keyword list.

The first event is free to enter. One hundred dollars in prizes, funded out of pocket, to see if this thing works before asking anyone else to spend.
